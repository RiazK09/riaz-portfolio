About Me
BACKGROUND

I didn’t start my career in tech.

My foundation began in construction and quantity surveying — a discipline built on structure, precision, and long-term planning. Over time, I realised I was more drawn to building digital systems than physical ones.

At 26, I made the decision to pivot into technology and became a Full Stack Web Developer.

Today, I serve as Senior Web Developer and Head of Web Development at StudioBotics, leading the delivery of high-performance business and e-commerce platforms.

In April, I will begin formal studies in Software Engineering to deepen my understanding of system architecture, scalability, and backend engineering — strengthening the technical foundation behind the work I already do.

My path hasn’t been conventional — but it’s shaped how I think, build, and lead.

And that perspective is reflected in every project I touch.

WhyMe
Developer | Technical Problem Solver | Project Manager

I've never been comfortable standing still. Whether it's learning new technologies, refining how systems are built, or improving how projects are delivered, I'm always pushing myself to become better at what I do.

My path into technology didn’t follow a conventional route. Starting in construction and project environments taught me to think in terms of structure, planning, and long-term durability — principles I carry into every digital platform I build.
